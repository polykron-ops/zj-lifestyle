# cl — item 5, three art-direction concepts (written; no generation)

David picks one at the gate; nothing is produced before. Constraints from AGENTS.md: no paid call, models fully AI-generated with no full faces, product fidelity first. Shares are from `reference/cl-template-plan.md`. Source-resolution shares are from `reference/cl-source-census.md`; the thresholds (1,500 px when the piece fills the frame, 1,000 px when it is mid-frame) are my assumptions, not tested.

## A. Object study (no person)
- **Setting:** a pale stone, linen or travertine surface with a single soft shadow; one prop at most (a leaf, a dish).
- **Crop:** tight, piece fills 50–70% of frame, square or 4:5.
- **Light:** window daylight from one side, long soft shadow, no reflections tuned to hide detail.
- **Model:** none.
- **How a piece sits:** laid, draped or propped; earrings as a pair, chains coiled in a shallow arc.
- **Carries:** every form, including the 1.1% not worn directly. **Cannot carry:** anything that sells fit or scale; it shows no wearer.
- **Fidelity risk:** lowest, since nothing is worn and no size is implied. **Source need:** large product in frame, so 1,625 of 2,478 sources (65.6%) clear 1,500 px; the other 853 are exposed.

## B. Worn crop (AI model, no face)
- **Setting:** neutral interior, linen and soft wall tones.
- **Crop:** jaw-down or profile-behind-the-ear; hands at waist for rings; wrist resting on a knee. The face is outside the frame or turned away.
- **Light:** warm natural side light, shallow depth of field.
- **Model:** present, fully generated, skin tone and styling to be set once and held constant.
- **How a piece sits:** worn, piece about 15–30% of frame.
- **Carries:** ear 35.0%, pendants and collars 27.9%, rings 21.1%, wrist 10.2%: about 94% of SKUs. **Cannot carry well:** station chains (4.5%), loose pendants and charms (1.1%).
- **Fidelity risk:** highest of the three: scale, drape and shine are invented; AI-person disclosure applies. **Source need:** mid-frame, so about 86.4% clear 1,000 px (338 do not).

## C. Scene in context (hands in a moment)
- **Setting:** a table, a cup, a window seat, a gift being opened; a world around the piece.
- **Crop:** wide, piece 5–10% of frame; only hands, wrists or an ear in a turned head are visible.
- **Light:** golden-hour or café light.
- **Model:** hands and partial figures only, no face.
- **How a piece sits:** small, in use.
- **Carries:** rings (21.1%), bracelets, cuffs and watches (10.2%): about 31%. **Cannot carry:** earrings and fine necklaces, which are lost at this scale.
- **Fidelity risk:** medium: small product hides detail but the scene implies use. **Source need:** lowest, since the piece is small.

**Why these are distinct:** A has no wearer and a large piece, B has a wearer and a medium piece, C has a story and a small piece. They differ in what carries the image (object, body, scene), not in styling.
