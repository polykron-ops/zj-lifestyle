# PLAN — zj-lifestyle

## Scope

A shared set of AI lifestyle images for ZJ's legacy catalogue, reused by visual product family rather than one image per SKU. Only SKUs introduced in the **last 3 years** are eligible. Target, subject to the numbers: 200 minimum, 400 to 600 recommended; pilot 25 to 30 approved images.

## State

Nothing built. No spend. The eligibility question is open and gates everything: if `introduction_date` cannot be recovered for most SKUs, the eligible population is unknown and the programme's size is unknown with it.

Known from cs's read-only inventory of the `zj1-images` Space (2026-11): 403,652 objects, 34.8 GB, 13 prefixes; a source photo map of 11,703 images covering 11,695 SKUs across the `saphirim` and `tamirzuman` catalogues; 11,694 cutout PNGs; delivery sizes 320/500/640/1000 px at 46,801 each, 9,531 at 1500 px, 8,098 at 1280 px. Not yet inspected: image content, quality, backgrounds, whether any are already lifestyle shots, SKU-to-published mapping, category and family distribution, introduction dates, and whether the best source per SKU suits compositing.

## Next

1. **Eligibility and the dataset** (cs, in flight): how many SKUs have a recoverable `introduction_date`, the one SKU-level CSV, families derived from the data rather than the navbar, a 30 to 50 SKU source sample, difficulty distribution, and the analysis that sizes the programme. No generation, US$0.
2. **David reads the numbers** and decides whether the programme proceeds, and at what scale.
3. **Art direction gate:** three materially distinct concepts, David picks one.
4. **Pilot:** 25 to 30 approved images against the chosen direction, with pass rate, retries, QA time and cost per approved image measured.
5. **Full programme**, if the pilot's economics justify it.
